Resources

Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD)

Atomic layer deposition is a thin-film deposition technique based on sequential, self-limiting surface reactions.

ALD provides precise thickness control, excellent film conformality, and high-quality deposition on complex three-dimensional structures.

The process is widely used in semiconductor manufacturing for dielectric films, diffusion barriers, and other functional thin-film materials.

X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS)

XPS is a surface-sensitive analytical technique used to determine elemental composition, oxidation states, and chemical bonding in materials.

It measures the kinetic energy of photoelectrons emitted when a material is irradiated with X-rays.

X-ray Diffraction (XRD)

X-ray diffraction is used to investigate crystallographic structure, phase composition, crystallinity, and preferred orientation in thin-film materials.

The technique uses constructive interference of X-rays from crystalline planes according to Bragg's law.

Spectroscopic Ellipsometry

Spectroscopic ellipsometry is a non-destructive optical technique used to determine thin-film thickness, refractive index, and extinction coefficient.

It analyzes changes in the polarization state of reflected light across a range of wavelengths.
